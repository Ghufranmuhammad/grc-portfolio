# Month 03 — Execution Plan

## Application Strategy

Target 5–8 genuine, targeted applications per week when suitable vacancies are available. Count an application only after actual submission.

**Target titles:** Junior GRC Analyst, GRC Intern, Cybersecurity GRC Intern, Compliance Analyst, IT Compliance Analyst, Junior Risk Analyst, IT Risk Analyst, Information Security Compliance Analyst, Junior Third-Party/Vendor Risk Analyst, ISO 27001 Auditor Trainee, and Cybersecurity Governance Trainee.

## Minimum Role-Fit Criteria

- Relevant role and responsibilities.
- Experience requirement not clearly disqualifying.
- Education requirement reviewed honestly.
- Location and work eligibility understood or flagged.
- One or two relevant portfolio evidence items identified.
- No false claims required.

## Resume Customization

1. Save the job description and URL.
2. Extract experience, education, skills, frameworks, and tools.
3. Select relevant projects.
4. Adjust the summary accurately.
5. Use keywords naturally.
6. Verify contact details and links.
7. Review for unsupported claims.
8. Submit and record only after actual submission.

## Follow-Up Schedule

- Day 0: Submit or send initial message.
- Day 4–5: Follow up when appropriate.
- Day 10–14: Final relevant follow-up.
- After Day 14: Stop repeated follow-ups unless there is a new reason.

## Skill Development

**Priority skill 1:** Control testing and evidence validation. Practice design versus operating effectiveness, evidence reliability, exceptions, conclusions, and remediation.

**Priority skill 2:** Risk assessment and treatment communication. Practice likelihood, impact, inherent/residual risk, treatment, ownership, deadlines, and escalation.

**Practical improvement:** Create or improve one educational control-testing or vendor-risk assessment, clearly labeled as a simulation.

## Interview Practice

Complete 3 sessions weekly. Answer at least one session without notes. Record confidence, technical gap, communication gap, corrective action, and retest date. Do not mark mastery merely because an answer looked familiar.

## GitHub Evidence

Potential additions: improved README, control-testing worksheet, evidence-validation checklist, risk treatment example, vendor-risk assessment, and resume-claim evidence index. Never upload confidential data or fabricated client evidence.

## Networking

- Recruiter outreach: 5 targeted contacts weekly.
- Professional networking: 2 relevant contacts weekly.
- Personalize by verifying relevance, mentioning a specific role/function, explaining the connection, including only relevant evidence, and asking one reasonable question.
- Follow up around Day 4–5 and Day 10–14 when appropriate; stop after clear lack of interest.

## Review System

**Weekly review day:** Sunday evening or another consistent day.

Track applications, role titles, recruiter contacts, networking contacts, responses, screenings, interviews, rejections and stated reasons, follow-ups, practice sessions, technical gaps, portfolio corrections, and learning hours.

Change strategy only when multiple relevant vacancies repeat a requirement, direct feedback repeats, role mismatch is recurring, interview practice shows a consistent gap, eligibility repeatedly blocks progress, or portfolio clarity is repeatedly questioned.

## Genuine Progress

Genuine progress includes accurate submissions, improved targeting, direct feedback, clearer project explanations, stronger answers without notes, completed practical exercises, respectful networking, accurate records, and correction of recurring weaknesses. None of these guarantees an employment outcome.
