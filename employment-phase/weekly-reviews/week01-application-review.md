## 1. Week 01 Objectives

The purpose of the first week is to establish a consistent and realistic job-search routine, submit carefully targeted applications, begin recruiter outreach, and prepare for entry-level GRC interviews.

The objective is quality and consistency rather than applying to as many jobs as possible. Every application should be based on a real vacancy, a suitable role, and a truthful match between the job requirements and my current skills.

## 4. Application Execution Plan

### Application 1 — Primary GRC Role

- Target: Junior GRC Analyst, GRC Analyst, or GRC Intern.
- Review the job description carefully.
- Identify required skills and frameworks.
- Match the role to relevant portfolio evidence.
- Adjust the resume only where necessary and truthful.
- Submit only if the vacancy is genuine and reasonably aligned.
- Record the submission in the job tracker.

### Application 2 — Compliance or Risk Role

- Target: Compliance Analyst, Junior Risk Analyst, or IT Compliance Analyst.
- Review documentation, risk, audit, and control requirements.
- Use relevant ISO 27001, enterprise risk, UAE NCA ECC, and control-testing evidence.
- Record gaps honestly.
- Submit and save confirmation or application records.

### Application 3 — Entry-Level or Internship Role

- Target: GRC Intern, Cybersecurity GRC Intern, Information Security Compliance Intern, or related trainee role.
- Prioritize learning opportunities and realistic entry requirements.
- Check whether the employer accepts candidates with educational projects and no formal GRC employment experience.
- Submit only after reviewing the complete vacancy.
- Record the result in the job tracker.

If suitable vacancies are unavailable, do not fabricate completed applications or apply indiscriminately. Use the time for company research, portfolio improvement, and identifying future opportunities.

## 5. Recruiter Contact Target

**Target: 5 targeted recruiter contacts**

Potential contacts include:

- GRC recruiters
- Cybersecurity recruiters
- Information security hiring specialists
- Technology risk recruiters
- Compliance and risk recruitment consultants
- Recruitment professionals working with UAE entry-level technology roles

### Contact Rules

- Verify that the person is relevant to the role or industry.
- Personalize each message.
- Do not send identical mass messages.
- Do not claim formal GRC experience.
- Mention relevant education and practical portfolio work.
- Record the date, person, company, message type, and response.
- Do not repeatedly contact the same person without a reason.

## 6. Interview-Practice Sessions

**Target: 3 sessions**

### Session 1 — Personal Introduction

Practice:

- Tell me about yourself.
- Why do you want to work in GRC?
- Why should we interview you without formal GRC experience?
- What are your career goals?

### Session 2 — Technical Foundations

Practice:

- What is GRC?
- What is the difference between a risk, threat, and vulnerability?
- Explain ISO 27001.
- What is a control?
- What is audit evidence?
- How would you document a risk?

### Session 3 — Portfolio and Behavioral Questions

Practice:

- Explain your ISO 27001 project.
- Explain your enterprise risk assessment.
- Describe a challenge you faced while building your portfolio.
- What would you do if you did not know the answer?
- How would you respond to feedback?

Each session should include speaking aloud rather than only reading answers.

## 9. Weekly Outcome Definition

A successful first week is not defined by receiving an interview invitation.

The week is successful if I:

- Submit up to 3 genuine, targeted applications.
- Contact 5 relevant recruiters or recruitment professionals.
- Complete 3 interview-practice sessions.
- Review 5 portfolio projects.
- Record all applications and outreach accurately.
- Identify repeated skill gaps or application problems.
- Complete one weekly review.
- Avoid exaggerating experience or marking incomplete activities as completed.
