## Month 02 Review

- How many roles closely matched my profile?
- How many recruiters did I contact?
- What responses did I receive?
- Were there screening or interview invitations?
- Which requirements appeared repeatedly?
- Which part of the process needs improvement?
- What direct feedback did I receive?
- What hypotheses can I test from no-response applications?
- What should change in Month 3?

A small sample without responses does not establish a definitive cause. Separate hypotheses from confirmed feedback.
