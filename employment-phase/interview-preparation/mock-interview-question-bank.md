# Mock Interview Question Bank

## GRC Fundamentals — 8

1. **What is GRC?** Governance sets direction and accountability; risk management identifies and treats uncertainty; compliance evaluates adherence to obligations and requirements. A junior analyst may support registers, evidence, reporting, and follow-up.
2. **Governance vs risk vs compliance?** Governance defines decisions and oversight; risk management evaluates uncertainty and treatment; compliance checks requirements. They overlap but are not identical.
3. **Threat, vulnerability, and risk?** A threat can cause harm, a vulnerability is a weakness, and risk considers likelihood and impact in context.
4. **What is a control?** A safeguard intended to prevent, detect, reduce, or correct risk. It may be administrative, technical, or physical.
5. **Policy, standard, and procedure?** A policy expresses management direction, a standard defines required conditions, and a procedure explains how work is performed.
6. **What does a junior GRC analyst do?** Maintain registers, collect evidence, support audits, track remediation, prepare reports, and communicate with control owners.
7. **Why is documentation important?** It creates traceability for requirements, owners, evidence, findings, actions, and decisions.
8. **Explain GRC to a business stakeholder.** GRC helps the organization make informed decisions, reduce avoidable risk, and demonstrate accountability in business terms.

## Risk Management — 8

9. **How do you identify a risk?** Understand the asset or process, identify the event, cause, consequence, existing controls, owner, likelihood, and impact.
10. **Likelihood vs impact?** Likelihood estimates probability; impact estimates consequence. Both should use approved criteria.
11. **Inherent risk?** Risk before considering controls or treatment.
12. **Residual risk?** Risk remaining after controls and treatment; it should be compared with risk appetite and escalated when necessary.
13. **Treatment options?** Avoid, reduce, transfer/share, or accept through authorized governance.
14. **Risk register contents?** ID, description, cause, consequence, owner, rating, controls, treatment, deadline, status, and residual risk.
15. **Owner disagrees with rating?** Explain criteria, hear context, review evidence, document disagreement, and escalate if needed; do not change it without justification.
16. **How prioritize risks?** Use approved criteria, impact, likelihood, criticality, regulatory exposure, data sensitivity, and control weakness.

## ISO 27001 / ISMS — 6

17. **What is ISO 27001?** A standard for establishing, implementing, maintaining, and improving an information security management system using a risk-based approach.
18. **What is an ISMS?** A management system covering scope, policies, risk assessment, treatment, responsibilities, operations, monitoring, audit, review, and improvement.
19. **Purpose of the SoA?** It records applicable Annex A controls, implementation status, and inclusion/exclusion justification.
20. **Gap assessment vs audit?** A gap assessment compares current state to requirements; an audit is a structured evidence-based evaluation against criteria. My work was not an official audit.
21. **Continual improvement?** Using findings, incidents, monitoring, reviews, and changing risks to improve the ISMS.
22. **Possible control evidence?** Approved policies, tickets, logs, access reviews, training records, risk assessments, supplier reviews, and test results, depending on the control.

## UAE Cybersecurity and Privacy — 6

23. **Purpose of a UAE cybersecurity framework?** To structure governance, protection, risk management, safeguards, and accountability within the applicable organizational and sector context.
24. **What is UAE NCA ECC?** A UAE cybersecurity control framework used in relevant contexts. My mappings were educational; the current version and scope must be verified professionally.
25. **What is personal data?** Information relating to an identified or identifiable individual, subject to the applicable legal definition.
26. **What is a DPIA?** A structured assessment of privacy risks associated with processing, especially higher-risk processing.
27. **What is a RoPA?** A record of processing activities documenting relevant purposes, data categories, subjects, recipients, retention, and safeguards where required.
28. **What if a privacy requirement is unclear?** Record the uncertainty and consult designated privacy, legal, or compliance stakeholders rather than making an unsupported legal conclusion.

## Control Testing and Evidence — 5

29. **What is control testing?** Evaluating whether a control is suitably designed and operates as expected during the relevant period.
30. **Design vs operating effectiveness?** Design asks whether the control can address the risk; operating effectiveness asks whether it actually operated consistently.
31. **Reliable evidence?** Relevant, authentic, traceable, timely, sufficiently complete, and connected to the control and testing period.
32. **Incomplete evidence?** Specify what is missing, request it with a deadline, document the limitation, and do not pass the control without sufficient support.
33. **What is a finding?** A documented exception, weakness, nonconformity, or improvement need against defined criteria, with evidence and follow-up information.
