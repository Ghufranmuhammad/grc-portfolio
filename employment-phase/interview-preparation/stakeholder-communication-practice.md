# Stakeholder Communication Practice

## 1. Control Owner — Missing Evidence
**Subject:** Evidence Request — [Control ID] — [Assessment Period]

Hello [Name],

As part of the current review, we are missing [specific evidence] for [control]. Could you please provide it by [date], or advise whether another team maintains it? This will help us complete the assessment accurately and avoid a conclusion without sufficient support.

Kind regards,
Gufran

## 2. Manager — Overdue High-Risk Remediation
**Subject:** Escalation — Overdue Remediation Item [ID]

Hello [Name],

I am escalating item [ID], which is overdue against [date]. The current status is [status], the owner is [owner], and the outstanding action is [action]. Please advise whether revised ownership, resources, a new deadline, or further escalation is appropriate. I will continue tracking the item and update the record when evidence is received.

Kind regards,
Gufran

## 3. Business Stakeholder — Security Concern
**Subject:** Security Consideration for [Process/Service]

Hello [Name],

During the review of [process/service], we identified a concern relating to [plain-language issue]. The potential business consequence is [consequence]. Confirmed facts are [facts], while [unknowns] require validation. The proposed next step is [action] by [date], with support from [team].

Kind regards,
Gufran
