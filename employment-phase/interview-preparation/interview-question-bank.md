Junior GRC Interview Question Bank

Purpose

This question bank contains 100 questions for entry-level GRC, risk, compliance, and information-security interviews.

The questions are organized into 10 categories with 10 questions each.

Interview Preparation Rule

Use the following answer structure:

Definition → Practical explanation → Example → Limitation or consideration

When discussing portfolio projects, always identify them as fictional educational simulations. Do not present simulated work as professional client experience or independent auditing experience.
