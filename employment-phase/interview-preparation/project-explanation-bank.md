Project Explanation Bank

How to Explain a Portfolio Project

Use this structure:

1. Business scenario
2. Objective
3. What I personally built
4. Evidence produced
5. What I learned
6. Limitation or next improvement

Always say "fictional educational simulation" when describing simulated organizational work.

---

1. ISO 27001 Gap Assessment

30-Second Explanation

I created a fictional organizational ISO 27001 gap-assessment project. I worked across Clauses 4–10 and Annex A concepts and produced a Statement of Applicability, risk treatment plan, control implementation tracker, and CAPA documentation. The purpose was to practice connecting ISMS requirements to risks, controls, evidence, and improvement actions.

Evidence

Repository: "Ghufranmuhammad/ISO27001-Gap-Assessment"

Evidence types:

- Gap assessment
- Statement of Applicability
- Risk treatment plan
- Control tracker
- CAPA documentation

Likely Follow-Up Questions

- What is the purpose of an ISMS?
- What is the Statement of Applicability?
- How did you determine whether a control was relevant?
- How would a real audit differ from your simulation?

Honest Limitation

This was an educational simulation and not a professional certification audit or client engagement.
