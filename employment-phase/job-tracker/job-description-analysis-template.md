Key Rule

Do not automatically reject yourself because a posting says "1–2 years experience."

Read the complete job description.

Compare the actual responsibilities and requirements with:

- Education
- Portfolio
- Framework knowledge
- Technical skills
- Transferable experience
- Communication skills
- Evidence of practical work

If enough of the core requirements are supported and the remaining gaps are realistic to learn, the role may still be worth applying to.

Always represent experience truthfully.
