Outreach Methodology

Objective

Build a small, targeted professional network within the UAE GRC, cybersecurity, risk and compliance market.

The goal is not to message hundreds of people.

The goal is to create relevant professional conversations and increase visibility for suitable entry-level opportunities.

---

1. Weekly Outreach Target

Initial target:

5–10 targeted contacts per week

Suggested starting point:

- 3–5 recruiters
- 2–5 hiring managers or relevant GRC/security/risk professionals

Quality is more important than volume.

Do not increase volume simply to reach a number.

---

2. How to Identify Relevant Recruiters

Search LinkedIn for recruiters associated with:

- Cybersecurity
- Information Security
- Technology
- IT
- Risk
- Compliance
- GRC
- Financial services
- Banking
- Consulting
- Telecommunications
- Technology companies
- Professional services

Useful recruiter titles include:

- IT Recruiter
- Technology Recruiter
- Cybersecurity Recruiter
- Information Security Recruiter
- Talent Acquisition Specialist
- Talent Acquisition Partner
- Technical Recruiter
- Recruitment Consultant
- Talent Acquisition Manager

Prioritize recruiters who appear to recruit for the UAE or for the industry/company relevant to the vacancy.

---

3. How to Identify Hiring Managers

Search for people working at the target company with titles such as:

- GRC Manager
- Information Security Manager
- Risk Manager
- Compliance Manager
- IT Risk Manager
- Cybersecurity Manager
- Information Security Director
- Head of GRC
- Head of Information Security
- Head of Risk
- CISO

For an entry-level GRC position, a direct manager or team leader may be more relevant than a very senior executive.

---

4. Verify the Person Before Contacting Them

Before sending a message, verify:

Company

Does their current LinkedIn profile show the same company as the vacancy?

Role

Does their role actually relate to:

- GRC
- Information security
- Risk
- Compliance
- Cybersecurity
- Talent acquisition/recruitment?

Location

If relevant, do they recruit for or work in the UAE?

Vacancy

If contacting someone about a specific position, confirm that the position actually exists on an official company careers page or a reputable job platform.

Profile consistency

Check that the person's current employer and role appear consistent across available professional information.

Do not assume someone is the hiring manager simply because their job title sounds senior.

---

5. How to Personalize Messages

Each message should contain at least one genuine reason for contacting that person.

Possible personalization:

- Their company has a relevant vacancy.
- They recruit for cybersecurity/GRC roles.
- They work in the relevant GRC/security/risk function.
- Their organization has an entry-level opportunity.
- You are applying to a specific position at their company.

Avoid fake personalization.

Do not write generic praise simply to make the message look personalized.

---

6. Message Structure

Use this structure:

Who I am → Why I'm contacting you → Relevant evidence → What I'm looking for → Portfolio

For a recruiter:

«Entry-level GRC candidate → relevant UAE opportunity → practical portfolio → suitable junior role → GitHub»

For a hiring manager:

«Entry-level GRC candidate → their relevant function/company → specific evidence → interest in suitable opportunity → portfolio»

---

7. What Evidence to Mention

Do not list the entire portfolio in every message.

Select evidence relevant to the recipient or vacancy.

Risk-focused role

Mention:

- 15+ risk register
- Risk ownership
- Likelihood/impact
- Risk treatment

ISO/compliance role

Mention:

- ISO 27001
- Clauses 4–10
- Annex A
- Statement of Applicability
- Risk treatment
- CAPA

UAE compliance role

Mention:

- UAE NCA ECC
- 20 control mappings
- 20 evidence items

Privacy role

Mention:

- PDPL
- Privacy governance
- DPIA
- RoPA

Controls role

Mention:

- Control objectives
- Testing procedures
- Evidence
- Findings
- Remediation

---

8. Do Not Oversell

Use:

- "educational simulation"
- "portfolio project"
- "practical portfolio"
- "hands-on learning"
- "applied my knowledge"

Avoid:

- "I have several years of GRC experience."
- "I have audited organizations."
- "I have implemented ISO 27001 professionally."
- "I am an experienced GRC consultant."
- "I have managed enterprise compliance programs."

The portfolio is evidence of practical learning, not a substitute for professional employment history.

---

9. Avoid Duplicate Outreach

Before contacting someone, check the outreach tracker.

Search by:

- Person
- Company
- Vacancy
- Date contacted

If another recruiter from the same company has already been contacted about the same vacancy, avoid contacting multiple people unless there is a legitimate reason.

If a recruiter has already responded, continue the conversation rather than starting a second outreach thread.

---

10. Record Every Contact

Track:

- Date
- Name
- LinkedIn URL
- Company
- Job title
- Recruiter / Hiring Manager
- Vacancy
- Vacancy URL
- Message type
- Date contacted
- Response
- Follow-up date
- Final status
- Notes

Possible statuses:

- Contacted
- Connected
- Awaiting Response
- Replied
- CV Requested
- Applied
- Screening
- Interview
- Rejected
- No Response
- Future Opportunity
- Do Not Contact

---

11. Handling No Response

No response does not necessarily mean rejection.

Follow the sequence:

Day 0 → Day 4–5 → Day 10–14 → Stop

After the final follow-up:

- Mark the contact as "No Response."
- Do not continue messaging.
- Continue searching for relevant vacancies.
- If a genuinely relevant new vacancy appears later, a new message may be appropriate.

---

12. Handling Rejection

If a recruiter rejects the application:

Respond professionally if a response is appropriate.

Do not argue or attempt to persuade them to reverse the decision.

If they provide feedback:

Record it.

Examples:

- Experience gap
- Certification gap
- Technical gap
- Industry experience
- Candidate selected
- Position closed
- Location
- Unknown

Use repeated feedback to identify genuine improvement areas.

---

13. Handling Recruiter Interest

If a recruiter responds positively:

1. Respond promptly.
2. Provide the requested CV.
3. Provide the portfolio link if useful.
4. Confirm the relevant role.
5. Ask only necessary questions.
6. Record the interaction.
7. Prepare for the role using the job-description analysis template.

If they request a call:

Prepare:

- 30-second introduction
- Qualification explanation
- Portfolio explanation
- Why GRC
- Why the company/role
- Availability
- Salary expectations if asked
- Work authorization/status information if relevant

Always answer honestly.

---

14. Privacy Protection

Do not send unnecessary personal information during initial outreach.

Do not send:

- Emirates ID number
- Passport number
- Bank details
- Full residential address
- Passwords
- Security codes
- Unnecessary identity documents

A CV should contain only information necessary for legitimate recruitment.

If an unfamiliar person requests sensitive documentation, verify the recruiter and company through an official channel before providing anything.

Do not publish sensitive personal information publicly on LinkedIn or GitHub.

---

Core Outreach Principle

The objective is:

Relevant person → Relevant role → Relevant evidence → Short message → Professional follow-up → Track result

Not:

100 messages → generic copy/paste → spam → no learning

A small number of well-researched contacts can provide better information and opportunities than a large volume of generic outreach.
