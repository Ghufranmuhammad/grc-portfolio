Recruiter Follow-Up Sequence

Objective

Maintain professional persistence without becoming repetitive or intrusive.

The standard sequence is:

Day 0 → Day 4–5 → Day 10–14 → Stop

Continue only if the recruiter responds or a legitimate new reason for contact appears.

---

Day 0 — Initial Contact

Send one of:

- Connection request
- Post-connection introduction
- Specific vacancy message

Keep the message concise.

Record:

- Date
- Recruiter
- Company
- Role
- Message type
- Vacancy URL
- Status

---

Day 4–5 — First Follow-Up

Use a short, professional follow-up.

Example:

Hello [Name],

Just following up on my previous message regarding potential entry-level GRC opportunities at [Company].

I remain interested in relevant junior opportunities and would be happy to provide my CV or portfolio if useful.

Thank you for your time.

Do not send another long explanation.

---

Day 10–14 — Final Follow-Up

Example:

Hello [Name],

I wanted to make one final follow-up regarding entry-level GRC opportunities at [Company].

I understand you may be busy, so no response is necessary if there is currently nothing suitable. If a relevant junior GRC, risk or compliance opportunity becomes available in the future, I’d be grateful to be considered.

Thank you again.

---

After Day 14 — Stop

Do not continue sending messages simply to get a response.

Stop outreach unless:

- The recruiter responds.
- A new relevant vacancy appears.
- The recruiter previously asked you to reconnect.
- There is a legitimate new development relevant to the conversation.

---

If the Recruiter Responds

Stop the automated sequence immediately.

Respond based on what they ask.

Possible outcomes:

"Send your CV."

Send the current role-appropriate CV.

"We don't have anything currently."

Thank them and ask to be considered for relevant future entry-level openings.

"You don't have enough experience."

Thank them for the feedback. Do not argue.

Record the feedback in the outreach tracker.

"Apply through the careers page."

Apply through the requested channel and, if appropriate, confirm that you have applied.

"We have an opening."

Move the opportunity into the job tracker and prepare specifically for the vacancy.

---

Anti-Spam Rules

Never:

- Send daily follow-ups.
- Send the same message repeatedly.
- Contact multiple employees at the same company about the same vacancy without a reason.
- Pressure a recruiter to respond.
- Ask repeatedly whether they have "any job."
- Continue contacting someone after they explicitly ask you to stop.

Professional persistence means following up appropriately and then respecting silence.
