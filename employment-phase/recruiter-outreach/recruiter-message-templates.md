Recruiter Message Templates

Purpose

Use these templates for targeted recruiter outreach.

The objective is to introduce myself professionally, demonstrate relevance, and make it easy for the recruiter to understand what I am targeting.

Do not send long messages.

Do not claim professional GRC experience that I do not have.

Personalization Rules

Before sending a message, replace generic information with something specific where available:

- [Name]
- [Company]
- [Job Title]
- [Relevant Framework]
- [Relevant Project]
- [Relevant Skill]

Do not personalize by inventing information about the recruiter.

Good personalization:

"I noticed you recruit for information-security roles at [Company]."

Better personalization:

"I noticed you are recruiting for the [Job Title] position at [Company], which matches my focus on entry-level GRC and risk."

Avoid:

"I've been following your amazing career for years."

unless this is genuinely true.

---

Outreach Rules

1. Keep connection requests short.
2. Do not attach a CV to the initial connection request unless the platform/recruiter specifically requests it.
3. Mention the portfolio when it is relevant.
4. Do not send the same generic message to dozens of people.
5. Never claim professional GRC experience when the experience is educational.
6. Do not pressure recruiters for a response.
7. Respect a clear "no."
8. Stop following up after the defined sequence unless there is a legitimate new reason to contact them.
